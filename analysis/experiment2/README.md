## Experiment 2: Molecular Dynamics Simulations ##
Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations evaluate pMHC complexes as flexible thermodynamic systems rather
than rigid static snapshots. Applying physics-based forcefields over time MD will correct structural
anomalies introduced by AF2m. i.e. models realistic physical chemistry

`OpenMM_pilot` tests the workflow (protonation, force-field parameterization, energy minimization)
